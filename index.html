About Us

About Dasha Auto
Refrigeration

Dasha Auto Refrigeration is an experienced automotive refrigeration and air-conditioning solutions provider with over 15 years of industry expertise. We specialize in delivering reliable, high-quality refrigeration and A/C systems for commercial vehicles across India.

From truck refrigeration units to bus A/C systems, cabin air-conditioning to container solutions — our comprehensive range of products and services is designed to meet the demanding requirements of temperature-sensitive transportation and commercial vehicle comfort.

Our commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted partner for businesses in cold-chain logistics, commercial transportation, and vehicle climate control.
